How FinAI Works

Three simple steps to assess your financial health without impacting your credit score.

01

Enter Financial Parameters

Provide your monthly income, living expenses, existing loan EMIs, and desired loan parameters. You can also use our 1-click Demo Fill button.

02

Automated BFSI Analysis

Our calculation engine applies FOIR thresholds, computes Debt-to-Income (DTI), checks credit tier contributions, and estimates maximum loan principal.

03

Actionable Insights & Guidance

Review your visual eligibility score, repayment amortization, and consult our Claude-powered AI assistant for tailored strategies to improve terms.

Built for Indian Borrowers & BFSI Transparency

FinAI was developed to bridge the information gap in retail lending. Applying blindly for multiple loans can trigger hard credit inquiries that pull down your CIBIL/Experian score. With FinAI, you can evaluate your eligibility, understand bank FOIR constraints, and plan smart repayments before stepping into a branch.
